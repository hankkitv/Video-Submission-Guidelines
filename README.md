# Creator Collaboration & Video Submission Guidelines

Welcome to our creator partnership program. Below are the standard guidelines and expectations for our collaboration. Please review this document carefully to ensure a smooth and successful process.

---

## 1. 📍 Restaurant Visit & Expenses
- **Independent Visit:** You will visit the designated location independently and manage your own video recording.
- **Upfront Payment:** You are responsible for covering your meal and related expenses upfront during the visit.
- **Reimbursement:** Upon submission of the completed video and a valid itemized receipt, we will reimburse your expenses up to **100,000 won**.

## 2. 🎥 Video Content & Expectations
- **Length:** There are no strict minimum or maximum duration requirements. Focus on quality and completeness rather than runtime.
- **Content Focus:** The goal is to produce an authentic, casual dining vlog that highlights:
  - The food and menu items
  - The establishment’s atmosphere and interior design
  - Your genuine, overall dining experience
- **Viewer Experience:** The final piece should make viewers feel as though they are visiting the location themselves, providing a clear and honest sense of what to expect.

## 3. ⚖️ Content Usage & Copyright Permission
- By participating in this program and accepting the expense reimbursement, you grant **E-HCS Co Ltd (a.k.a HankkiTV)** the permission to edit, publish, and distribute your video content across our official channels.
- *Note:* While we reserve the right to edit, our standard practice is to publish submitted videos in their original form with minimal to no alterations, preserving your unique creative voice.

## 4. 📤 Video Submission Process
Please submit your completed content using one of the following methods:
1. **Direct File Transfer:** Send the final video file directly via your preferred file-sharing method (e.g., Google Drive, Dropbox).
2. **Platform Link:** Upload the video to your own Youtube channel (as Public or Unlisted) and share the direct URL with us.

> **⚠️ Important:** Regardless of the submission method, you **must** include a clear copy or photo of your itemized restaurant receipt to initiate the reimbursement process.

## 5. 💼 Payment & Tax Documentation
To ensure compliant and timely payment, the required documentation depends on your current professional affiliation:

- **Represented by an Agency or Management Company:**  
  Your agency must issue a formal tax invoice or billing statement to **E-HCS Co Ltd**. We will process the reimbursement payment directly to your agency, and you will coordinate with them to receive your funds.

- **Independent Creator / Freelancer:**  
  You will need to provide valid Alien Registration Card (ARC) along with your official banking details. This allows us to process the payment and handle any required tax reporting on your behalf in accordance with local regulations.

---

*If you have any questions regarding these guidelines, the submission process, or payment details, please do not hesitate to reach out.*
