## Creator Collaboration & Video Submission Guidelines

Welcome to our creator partnership program!

We invite creators to share their authentic dining experiences at selected restaurants in Korea. Our goal is to introduce interesting dining destinations through genuine, engaging, and relatable videos.

You do not need professional filming equipment or advanced editing skills. We value authenticity, creativity, and a good representation of the overall dining experience.

Please review the following guidelines before participating.

### 1. Restaurant Visit & Expenses
Independent Visit: You will visit the designated restaurant independently and manage your own video recording.
Upfront Payment: You are responsible for covering your meal and related expenses upfront during the visit.
Reimbursement: We will reimburse your actual eligible expenses, up to a maximum of ₩100,000, upon receiving your completed video and a valid itemized restaurant receipt.
Payment Timeline: Reimbursement will be processed within 24 hours after we receive both your completed video and valid itemized receipt.
Publication Is Not a Payment Condition: Reimbursement is not dependent on whether HankkiTV ultimately chooses to publish your video, provided the submission meets the stated requirements.
### 2. Video Content & Expectations
#### Video Length

There are no strict minimum or maximum duration requirements. Focus on quality and completeness rather than runtime.

#### Content Focus

The goal is to produce an authentic, casual dining vlog that highlights:

Food & Menu: Showcase the food, menu items, presentation, and dining options.
Restaurant Atmosphere: Capture the establishment's atmosphere, interior design, and overall environment.
Dining Experience: Share your genuine experience and impressions of the restaurant.
Viewer Experience: Help viewers feel as though they are visiting the location themselves, providing a clear and honest sense of what to expect.
Authenticity & Editorial Guidelines
We welcome genuine opinions and honest feedback.
Videos should provide a reasonably balanced representation of the restaurant and overall dining experience.
Videos that exclusively focus on negative aspects of the restaurant, without meaningful coverage of the overall dining experience, will not be accepted.
Constructive criticism and genuine negative observations are welcome. We do not require exclusively positive reviews or ask creators to conceal legitimate concerns.

#### HankkiTV reserves the right to decline submissions that do not meet these content guidelines.

### 3. Video Quality & Equipment Requirements

We want authentic and enjoyable restaurant experiences, not professional film productions.

#### Minimum Video Quality

The minimum acceptable visual quality should be comparable to clear, well-recorded smartphone footage.

4K video recording at 24, 30, or 60 frames per second (fps).
1080p Full HD recording at 30 or 60 fps.
Optical image stabilization for video recording.
Continuous autofocus.

However, recording in 4K is NOT required. Clear 1080p Full HD footage is perfectly acceptable.

#### Practical Recording Guidelines
- Ensure the food and restaurant surroundings are clearly visible.
- Use sufficient lighting so that footage is not excessively dark.
- Keep the camera reasonably steady and maintain proper focus.
- Avoid excessively blurry, shaky, or distorted footage.
- Ensure that audio is reasonably clear whenever narration or conversation is included.
#### Equipment
- No high-end camera or professional filming equipment is required.
- Modern smartphones are perfectly suitable for this project.
- Professional lighting, microphones, tripods, and advanced editing software are not necessary.
- Basic video editing is sufficient.

Our goal is to capture an authentic dining experience, not to produce a commercial-quality advertisement.

### 4. Video Submission Deadline

Once your participation has been approved, you will have 30 calendar days (one month) to complete your restaurant visit and submit your video.

If you anticipate needing additional time, please contact us in advance to discuss a possible extension.

We respect the creative ownership and independence of our contributors.

All intellectual property rights and ownership of submitted videos remain with the original creator.

Participating in this program does not transfer ownership of your work to HankkiTV.

This license applies exclusively to the actual video content submitted for this collaboration.

The creator's personal social media accounts or other online content.

YouTube

Video compression.

However, submitted videos will not be edited in a way that changes their original context, meaning, or intended message.

Our intention is to preserve the creator's original perspective and creative expression.

### 6. Video Submission Process

Please submit your completed content using one of the following methods:

#### Option 1: Direct File Transfer

Send the final video file directly using your preferred file-sharing service, such as:

Google Drive
Dropbox
Other mutually agreed file-transfer services.
#### Option 2: Platform Link

Upload your completed video to your own YouTube channel as either:

Public
Unlisted

Then share the direct video URL with us.

Regardless of the submission method, you must include a clear copy or photograph of your itemized restaurant receipt to initiate the reimbursement process.

Please ensure that any shared video links and files remain accessible until the submission and reimbursement process is complete.

### 7. Payment & Tax Documentation

To ensure compliant and timely payment, the required documentation depends on your current professional affiliation.

#### Represented by an Agency or Management Company

Your agency must issue a formal tax invoice or billing statement to E-HCS Co Ltd.

We will process the reimbursement payment directly to your agency, and you will coordinate with them to receive your funds.

#### Indepvendent Creator / Freelancer

You will need to provide:

A valid Alien Registration Card (ARC).
Your official banking details.

This information is required to facilitate payment processing and any applicable tax reporting in accordance with local regulations.

Personal information provided for payment and tax documentation will be handled for those purposes in accordance with applicable privacy and data protection requirements.
